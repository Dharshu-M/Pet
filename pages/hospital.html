Before You Arrive

What To Expect On A Hospital Visit

  • Bring any past reports, current medication, and vaccination records.
  • A vet nurse takes weight, temperature, and vitals before the doctor sees you.
  • You'll get a written summary and next steps before you leave.
  • Follow-up calls for surgical or overnight cases, so you're never left wondering.
Walk-ins Welcome

Emergencies are seen immediately, no appointment required. For routine checkups, booking ahead means a shorter wait.
